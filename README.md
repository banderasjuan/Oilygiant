# 🛢️ Oil Well Location Optimization & Investment Risk Analysis

## Project Overview
Advanced statistical analysis and machine learning system for optimal oil well location selection, implementing comprehensive risk assessment and financial modeling to maximize ROI while minimizing investment risk across multiple geographical regions.

## Business Context
An oil & gas company needed to select the most profitable region for new oil well development from three potential locations. The decision involved multi-million dollar investments requiring rigorous statistical analysis to minimize financial risk while maximizing expected returns.

## Industry Challenge
Oil & gas exploration involves significant capital investment with uncertain returns:
- **High Capital Requirements**: $50-100M+ per drilling project
- **Geological Uncertainty**: Unknown reserve quantities until drilling
- **Regional Variations**: Different geological characteristics per location
- **Risk Management**: Need for statistical confidence in investment decisions

## Methodology & Approach
- **Multi-Regional Analysis**: Systematic comparison of 3 geographical regions
- **Predictive Modeling**: Linear regression for reserve estimation
- **Risk Assessment**: Bootstrap statistical methods for confidence intervals
- **Financial Modeling**: Profit calculation and ROI analysis per well
- **Statistical Validation**: Percentile analysis and probability distributions

## Technical Implementation

### Core Analytics Framework
1. **Geological Data Analysis**: Processing seismic and geological survey data
2. **Reserve Estimation**: ML models predicting oil reserves per location
3. **Bootstrap Risk Analysis**: 1000+ simulations for statistical confidence
4. **Profit Modeling**: Custom functions for revenue and cost calculations
5. **Decision Framework**: Risk-adjusted recommendations for executive decisions

### Custom Functions Developed
- **`model_review()`**: Comprehensive model evaluation across regions
- **`ganancia()`**: Profit calculation per well with cost considerations
- **`boot()`**: Bootstrap sampling for risk assessment
- **`estadisticas()`**: Statistical summary and confidence interval calculation
